Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a reading?

Tap the blue Add Reading button at the bottom of the home screen. Enter your systolic and diastolic values and tap Save.

How do I delete a reading?

Swipe left on any reading in the list and tap Delete.

How do I export my readings to PDF?

Tap the share icon in the top right corner of the home screen. Choose your date range and chart style then tap Export PDF.

Will my data be lost if I update the app?

No. All readings are stored locally on your device and persist through app updates.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

Unfortunately no. Since all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all readings. We recommend exporting a PDF before uninstalling.

What do the colour zones on the chart mean?

The colours follow the American Heart Association (AHA) guidelines:

  • Blue — Low (below 90/60)
  • Green — Normal (below 120/80)
  • Yellow — Elevated (120–129)
  • Orange — High Stage 1 (130–139)
  • Red — High Stage 2 or above (140+)

Is my health data safe?

Yes. BP Monitor stores all data locally on your device only. No data is ever sent to any server or shared with any third party.
