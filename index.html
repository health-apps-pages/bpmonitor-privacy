BP Monitor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

BP Monitor is a blood pressure tracking app built for personal health monitoring. Your privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

BP Monitor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All blood pressure readings, notes, and measurements you enter are stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your iPhone using UserDefaults. No data is ever sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. Your readings never leave your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics, no advertising networks, no crash reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

PDF Export

The PDF export feature generates a report entirely on your device. The file is shared only when you explicitly choose to share it using the iOS share sheet. We have no access to any exported files.

Health Data

BP Monitor does not connect to or read from Apple Health (HealthKit). Your blood pressure data is stored only within the app and is not accessible to any other app or service.

Children's Privacy

BP Monitor does not collect any data from any users including children. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
